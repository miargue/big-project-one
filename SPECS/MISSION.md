# Mission

## What is this?

Budget Basket is a grocery spending tracker for students, families, and anyone trying to manage their grocery budget.

## Why does it exist?

People often lose track of how much they spend on groceries and end up going over budget. Budget Basket helps users record purchases, see where their money goes, and stay within their spending limits.

## Who is it for?

Students, families, and anyone living on a budget who wants to be more mindful about grocery spending.

## What does success look like?

- Users can quickly record a grocery purchase
- Users can see all their purchases organized by week, month, or trip
- Users can set a budget and know exactly how much they have left to spend
- The app feels simple and easy to use

## What is out of scope?

- No user accounts or login (single-user app)
- No complex features like receipts, barcodes, or price comparisons
